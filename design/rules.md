# Final Titan Rules

## What the Game Is About

Each player recruits a team of Titans. Titans enter the arena to fight one-on-one dice duels. Choose when to fight, use Upgrade cards to support your Titans, and defeat the other players' teams. The last player with a Titan remaining becomes the Final Titan and wins.

These rules describe the standard game, followed by its optional and alternate modes. Solo & Co-op Mode has additional rules in [final-titan-coop-rules.md](final-titan-coop-rules.md).

## Components

The base game contains 100 Titan cards, 50 Upgrade cards, 35 Status tokens, 38 Life tokens, 5 Champion/Challenger tokens, 2 Charge tokens, 4 Team Power tokens, 4 Ultimate Form tokens, 2 dice, and 5 Overview cards. Tokens are not limited: use a suitable replacement if the supply runs out.

## Titan Cards

A Titan card shows its name, maximum Life, Speed, and, when present, Armor. The card also shows its Attack Chart, abilities, Ultimate, and possibly a Team Power and Ultimate Form. Advanced Titans are marked as such.

## Golden Rules

1. If a card directly contradicts these rules, follow the card.
2. If one effect says something happens and another says it cannot happen, the "cannot" effect takes precedence.
3. Resolve as much of an effect as possible. Ignore only the parts that cannot be performed.

## Winning and Losing

If all Titans belonging to a player are defeated, that player is eliminated. They discard all cards remaining in their hand. The last player remaining wins.

A fight is a one-on-one duel between the Champion and the Challenger. The player whose Titan defeats the other Titan wins that fight and normally receives a victory reward.

## Game Setup

1. Shuffle the Titan cards and Upgrade cards separately. Place each as a face-down deck and leave room beside each deck for a discard pile.
2. Put the tokens by type within reach of all players and place the two dice nearby.
3. Deal four Upgrade cards to each player.
4. Place the top card of the Upgrade deck face down in the arena.
5. For a first game, use only Standard Titans and Standard Upgrade cards. These do not use Blessed, Cursed, or Minion tokens. Add Advanced cards after learning the game.

## Drafting Titans

For a first game, deal three random Titans to each player instead of drafting.

For a draft, place three Titans face up in the middle of the table. The youngest player chooses first; play then proceeds to the left. On a turn, choose one of the three face-up Titans and place it face up in front of you. Immediately replace it from the Titan deck, so there are always three face-up choices. Continue until every player has three Titans. The Titans in front of a player form that player's bench.

Resolve the Start of Game abilities of all Titans. If the order matters, the player whose Titan has the higher Speed resolves their ability first. If tied, determine the order randomly.

The first player is the player whose Titan has the highest Speed. If tied, compare those players' next-highest Speed values, then continue with their next-highest values as needed. If all compared values are tied, choose the first player randomly among the tied players.

Titans on a bench do not have Life tokens and cannot take damage. They can gain Status tokens. Life tokens are used only for Titans in the arena.

## The Arena

When a Titan enters the arena, take Life tokens equal to its maximum Life and place them beside it. Give it a Charge token with the gray side up and a Champion/Challenger token showing its role.

The first Titan to enter an empty arena becomes the Champion. The next Titan to enter becomes the Challenger. A Champion does not choose SKIP or FIGHT during the next Phase 1.

## The Three Phases

A game repeats these phases until only one player remains:

1. Skip or Fight
2. Upgrade
3. Combat

### Phase 1: Skip or Fight

Starting with the first player, players take turns clockwise. On your turn, choose SKIP or FIGHT. If you have no Upgrade cards in your hand, or only two players remain in the game, you must choose FIGHT.

**SKIP:** Choose one Upgrade card from your hand and place it face down in the arena. The next player to your left chooses SKIP or FIGHT.

**FIGHT:** Choose one Titan from your bench and send it into the arena. Take all face-down Upgrade cards in the arena and add them to your hand.

If your Titan enters an empty arena, it becomes Champion. The next player to your left continues Phase 1; the Champion does not take part in this choice. Continue around the table until another Titan enters and becomes Challenger. If a Champion was already in the arena when you chose FIGHT, your Titan becomes Challenger. Once two Titans are in the arena, continue to Phase 2.

### Phase 2: Upgrade

Only the two players in the current fight take part in this phase and in Combat.

The player whose Titan has the higher Speed is the starting player. If their Speed values are tied, the Challenger's player is the starting player.

1. The starting player may play any number of Upgrade cards face down beside their Titan. The other player may then do the same.
2. Reveal all played Upgrade cards at the same time.
3. If an Upgrade changes a Titan's Speed, determine the starting player again after the Upgrade cards are revealed. The player whose Titan now has the higher Speed becomes the starting player; if tied, the Challenger's player starts.
4. The starting player resolves the When Revealed effects on their Upgrade cards in any order, then the other player does the same.
5. The starting player resolves their Titan's and Upgrade cards' Start of Fight abilities in any order, then the other player does the same.

Upgrade cards give abilities only for the duration of this fight. They are normally discarded when the fight ends. A card may state otherwise.

### Phase 3: Combat

The starting player takes the first turn. Players then alternate turns. On your turn, resolve all of your Titan's effects that occur at the start of your turn. If there are several, choose their order. Poison and Fire tokens also cause effects at this time.

You normally take one Action on your turn. Action tokens and other effects can give you additional Actions, including during the turn in which you gain them. If you have unspent Actions when your turn would otherwise end, take another Action. Continue until you have used all your Actions.

To take an Action, roll one die. Resolve the matching result on your Titan's Attack Chart, first resolving its primary effect and then its secondary effect, if any. If the foe is defeated, the fight ends immediately; do not resolve remaining effects.

#### Miss

The blank side is a Miss. It normally has no effect. A Miss still counts as a roll unless an effect says it is ignored entirely. If a roll is ignored entirely, it does not resolve or trigger other effects.

#### Charge and Ultimate

Each Titan begins a fight with its Charge token gray side up. When you roll Charge while it is gray side up, turn it to its colored side and take another Action.

When you roll Charge while the token is colored side up, trigger your Titan's Ultimate immediately. Resolve its effects one after another in the order written, then turn the Charge token gray side up. You do not take another Action for that Charge result.

If the Ultimate includes an Ultimate Form icon, take an Ultimate Form token from the supply and place it on the Titan card. The Ultimate Form ability remains active while that Titan remains in the game. If the Titan already has its Ultimate Form token, do not take another; resolve the other Ultimate effects as usual.

#### Winning a Fight and Victory Reward

A Titan is defeated when it loses all its Life tokens or an effect defeats it. When you defeat the foe, you win the fight and draw one Upgrade card from the deck.

If the Upgrade deck runs out, shuffle its discard pile to form a new deck.

#### Fight Clean-up

The defeated Titan goes to the discard pile. Return its Life tokens, Status tokens, and Ultimate Form token to the supply. Discard its face-up Upgrade cards.

The winning Titan stays in the arena as Champion. It keeps its current Life; it does not heal back to maximum Life. It keeps its Status tokens except Fire tokens, and keeps its Ultimate Form token. Remove its Fire tokens, discard its played Upgrade cards, and turn its Charge token gray.

The next player is the one sitting to the left of the previous Challenger. If that player also fought in the previous fight as Champion, the next player is the one to their left instead. The Champion does not participate in the next Phase 1. Return to Phase 1.

## Icons and Status Tokens

Status tokens are taken from the supply and placed beside a Titan. They normally remain with that Titan between fights and there is no limit to how many a Titan may have. An effect that gives Status tokens is mandatory unless the card says otherwise. It may affect your Titan, the foe, or a Titan you choose on a bench or in the arena.

### Armor

A Titan without an Armor value has Armor 0. Armor reduces damage by its value each time damage is dealt. It does not reduce damage from that Titan's Poison or Fire tokens. Armor can be increased by Armor tokens or by an ability that changes the Titan's Armor value.

Each Armor token increases Armor by 1.

### Poison

The Poison icon gives the foe one Poison token. It does not deal damage and is not affected by Armor, Dodge, or Minion tokens.

At the start of each of its turns, a Titan takes damage equal to the number of Poison tokens it has. This damage is taken once as a single amount and is not reduced by Armor, Dodge, or Minion tokens.

### Fire

The Fire icon gives the foe one Fire token. It does not deal damage and is not affected by Armor, Dodge, or Minion tokens.

At the start of each of its turns, a Titan takes damage equal to its Fire tokens, then gains one additional Fire token. Fire-token damage is taken as one amount and is not reduced by Armor, Dodge, or Minion tokens. Fire tokens are removed at the end of each fight and do not carry over.

### Heal

A Titan that heals gains the indicated number of Life tokens, but cannot exceed its maximum Life.

### Special Move

The Special Move icon triggers the Special Move shown on the Titan card.

### Defeat

The defeat icon defeats the foe immediately. It does not deal damage and is not prevented by Armor, Dodge, or Minion tokens.

### Team Power

A Titan's Team Power is active while that Titan is in the arena or on its bench. It affects its whole team, including itself. If you participate in a fight and have a Titan with a Team Power in the arena or on your bench, place a Team Power token beside the arena as a reminder. In a fight, only the two participating players' Team Powers apply.

### Drain

When a Titan deals damage with Drain, it heals by the same amount. Damage increases and reductions also affect Drain. You cannot drain more Life from the foe than it currently has, or heal above your own maximum Life. If the damage is canceled, or the foe prevents it with Dodge or Minion, no Life is drained.

### Power

Each Power token increases all damage dealt by that Titan by 1. Power tokens stack, and the increased damage is dealt at once. They increase damage from abilities as well as Attack Chart results. They do not affect effects that do not deal damage. If an Ultimate has multiple effects, resolve each effect separately and apply Power to each effect that deals damage.

### Cancel

Some abilities cancel a specific die roll or icon of the foe. A canceled die roll is ignored entirely, including all effects and additional icons from that roll. If an effect cancels only a particular icon within a multi-icon effect, resolve the other icons normally. A canceled roll does not trigger other effects.

### Blessed and Cursed

A Titan with a Blessed or Cursed token rolls two dice instead of one whenever it takes an Action. With Blessed, its player chooses which die to resolve. With Cursed, the foe's player chooses. Ignore the other die entirely.

A Titan cannot have both tokens or more than one of either token. If it would gain a second token of the same kind, it keeps the token it already has. If it would gain the opposite kind, remove the token it has instead. If it rolls doubles, resolve only one die and remove its Blessed or Cursed token before resolving that die.

### Action

The Action icon gives the Titan another Action. Each Action token gives it one additional Action on each of its turns. Action tokens apply during the turn in which they are gained.

### Dodge

If a Titan with a Dodge token would take damage, it must lose one Dodge token instead and takes no damage from that instance. This is mandatory. Dodge does not prevent effects that are not damage, and cannot prevent damage from the Titan's own Poison or Fire tokens.

### Minion

If a Titan with a Minion token would be defeated by one instance of damage, it must lose one Minion token instead and takes no damage from that instance. This is mandatory. Minion does not prevent effects that are not damage, and cannot prevent damage from the Titan's own Poison or Fire tokens.
If a Titan with a Minion token would be defeated by one instance of damage, it must lose one Minion token instead and takes no damage from that instance. This is mandatory. Minion does not prevent effects that are not damage, and cannot prevent damage from the Titan's own Poison or Fire tokens. If one instance of damage would defeat a Titan that has both Dodge and Minion tokens, it must lose a Dodge token and keep its Minion token.

## Special Cases and Clarifications

### Returning to the Bench

A Titan that returns to its bench heals to maximum Life. Its Life tokens return to the supply. It loses its face-up Upgrade cards and current Charge, and loses Fire tokens, but keeps its other Status tokens. The opposing Titan remains alone in the arena, wins the fight, and receives the victory reward unless a card says otherwise.

### Swapping Titans

A Titan that swaps with a Titan on its bench returns to its bench and heals to maximum Life. Its Life tokens return to the supply. It loses its current Charge and Fire tokens, but keeps its other Status tokens. The Titan entering the arena continues the same fight in the same role and uses the same face-up Upgrade cards. It is not a new fight, so Start of Fight abilities do not trigger again.

### Simultaneous Defeat

If both Titans are defeated at the same time, both lose the fight and no one receives the victory reward. If this happens in the final fight with only those two players left, the official FAQ says each player draws a Titan at random from the remaining face-down Titan deck, resolves any Start of Game ability, and plays one final fight.

### Resolving Damage

If several effects change one instance of damage, resolve them in this order:

1. Increase the damage, including Power tokens and abilities that increase damage.
2. Resolve Cancel.
3. Reduce the damage by Armor.
4. Apply effects that replace the amount of damage taken.
5. Use Dodge or Minion tokens.

Poison and Fire-token damage is not reduced by Armor, Dodge, or Minion tokens.

When an effect says "for each," count the matching items and resolve the total as one instance.

### Effects at the Same Time

When effects trigger at the same time, the player whose turn it is resolves all of their effects first, in any order, then the other player resolves theirs.

A triggered effect that interrupts another effect resolves immediately. After the interruption, finish resolving the effect that was interrupted. If a Titan is defeated, the fight ends immediately and any remaining effects are not resolved.

### Questions About Rules

When an unusual interaction is not covered by the rules, make a quick judgment so the game can continue. Check the publisher's FAQ after the game if needed.

## Optional Rule: Betting

Players may agree to use Betting. It adds a phase after Skip or Fight and before Upgrade. Players whose Titans are in the arena do not bet. Every other player may bet on the Champion, bet on the Challenger, or choose not to bet. You cannot bet if you have no Upgrade cards in your hand.

Place the Champion/Challenger token under your hand with your choice hidden. Reveal all bets together. At the end of the fight, a correct bet earns one Upgrade card; an incorrect bet requires discarding one Upgrade card. If both Titans are defeated at once, no one won the fight, so all bets are incorrect.

## Two-Player Mode (1 vs 1)

Use the standard rules with these changes:

- Deal five Upgrade cards to each player.
- Draft four Titans for each player.
- On every turn in Phase 1, a player must choose FIGHT and cannot choose SKIP.
- Do not use Betting.

Phase 2 and Phase 3 are unchanged.

## 2 vs 2 Mode

Form two teams of two. Teammates sit beside each other. Deal four Upgrade cards to each player. Teammates may discuss strategy but may not reveal or describe the cards in their hands. Draft three Titans for each player; teammates may discuss their draft choices.

Players must always choose FIGHT and cannot choose SKIP. When a Titan is defeated, the team chooses which Titan enters next. The team may choose from either teammate's bench.

Both teammates may play any number of Upgrade cards from their respective hands to support the Titan in the arena. Play them face down and reveal them together; discard them at the end of the fight. Team Powers belonging to either teammate's Titans apply to both teammates' Titans. The benches remain separate. There is no Betting. A team wins when all Titans belonging to the opposing team are defeated. A player with no Titans is eliminated and discards their remaining Upgrade cards.
Both teammates may play any number of Upgrade cards from their respective hands to support the Titan in the arena. Play them face down and reveal them together; discard them at the end of the fight. Team Powers belonging to either teammate's Titans apply to both teammates' Titans. For card effects that refer to "my team" or "all Titans on my team," both teammates' Titans count as one team. The benches remain separate. There is no Betting. A team wins when all Titans belonging to the opposing team are defeated. A player with no Titans is eliminated and discards their remaining Upgrade cards.

## Godspark Expansion: Legendary Upgrade Cards

Place the Legendary Upgrade cards in a separate deck. Every Titan with the "Legendary Upgrade" keyword uses its matching Legendary Upgrade.

When resolving Start of Game abilities, find the matching Legendary Upgrade and attach it to that Titan. It stays attached between fights until the Titan is defeated. If that Titan wins a fight, its Legendary Upgrade stays attached.

When the Titan is defeated, the player who won the fight takes its Legendary Upgrade into their hand. It then counts as a regular Upgrade card: it may be used to SKIP or played on a Titan for one fight, then is discarded as usual. If a Legendary Upgrade is discarded for any reason, return it to the Legendary Upgrade deck.
