# Final Titan: Solo & Co-op Mode

## The Mode

Play alone or with one or more teammates against an enemy team of Titans. Defeat every enemy Titan, one after another, to win together. Enemy Upgrade cards remain active and build up after each fight, making later fights more difficult. If all Titans belonging to all players are defeated, everyone loses immediately.

Learn the basic rules of Final Titan before playing Solo & Co-op. Use the general rules in [rules.md](rules.md), except where this document changes them. There is no Betting in Solo & Co-op.

## Game Setup

Deal four Upgrade cards to each player. Teammates may discuss strategy, but may not reveal or describe cards in their hands. Do not place an Upgrade card face down in the arena during setup.

Choose the number of players and difficulty. Take the indicated number of random Titan cards to make the enemy team's face-down Titan deck:

| Difficulty | 1 player | 2 players | 3 players |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Easy | 2 | 4 | 6 |
| Standard | 3 | 5 | 7 |
| Hard | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| Very Hard | 5 | 7 | 9 |
| Insane | 6 | 8 | 10 |

For every Titan in the enemy team, deal one random Upgrade card into a separate face-down enemy Upgrade deck. This is the enemy team's hand.

Draft Titans as usual until each player has three Titans on their bench. Resolve Start of Game abilities as usual.

## Phase 1: Skip or Fight

The enemy team supplies the first Champion. Reveal the top Titan of the enemy deck and place it in the arena. Reveal the next enemy Titan and place it on the enemy bench; its Team Power is active while it is on that bench.

Reveal the top card of the enemy Upgrade deck and place it beside the enemy Champion. It remains active for the rest of the game and upgrades every enemy Titan you fight. It is not discarded at the end of a fight.

If the Champion is an enemy Titan, the players do not choose SKIP or FIGHT. Decide together which player sends a Titan from their bench into the arena as Challenger.

If the Champion belongs to a player, the next enemy Titan in the enemy queue enters the arena as Challenger. When no enemy Titan remains on the enemy bench, reveal the next Titan from the enemy deck and place it on the bench for the next fight. Continue drawing and sending enemy Titans in queue order.

If an effect makes an enemy Titan return to its bench, place it at the front of the enemy queue. It is not defeated. It will be the next enemy to enter the arena. Do not reveal a new enemy Upgrade for a Titan returning to the bench. Reveal the next enemy Titan from its deck only when the enemy bench is empty.

## Phase 2: Upgrade

All players may play any number of Upgrade cards from their hands to support the Titan chosen for the fight. Players may play their cards in any order. Place them face down, then reveal all of them together. These Upgrade cards are discarded at the end of the fight, as usual.

Resolve the fight using Phase 3: Combat in [rules.md](rules.md). Team Powers from Titans on all teammates' benches apply to the player Titan in the arena. Enemy Team Powers on the enemy bench are also active.

## Phase 3: Combat and Enemy Upgrades

Fight using the standard Combat rules. If the players win, the player who won the fight draws one Upgrade card as the normal victory reward.

Then reveal the top card of the enemy Upgrade deck and add it beside the other face-up enemy Upgrade cards. It remains active until the end of the game and upgrades every enemy Titan in later fights. Enemy Upgrade cards accumulate; do not discard them during fight clean-up.

If only one enemy Titan remains for the upcoming final fight, reveal and add every Upgrade card still in the enemy team's hand instead of revealing just one. If no cards remain in that hand, reveal none.

Return to Phase 1 and continue until the players defeat all enemy Titans or all players' Titans have been defeated.

## Enemy-Team Clarifications

- If the enemy team would have to make a decision, the players choose the option most in favor of the enemy.
- Some Titans and Upgrade cards have a special mark in the lower-right corner showing that they cannot be used by the enemy team. If one is revealed for the enemy, discard it and replace it with a random card from the main supply.
- Each teammate has their own bench. Teammates do not share benches.
- If an effect gives the enemy team additional Upgrade cards, add them face down to the enemy team's hand.
- A newly revealed Titan with the Legendary Upgrade keyword receives its matching Legendary Upgrade immediately.
- A player with no Titans remaining is eliminated and discards their remaining Upgrade cards. The group loses only when all players' Titans have been defeated.

## Rulebook Challenge: First Steps

Prepare the enemy Titan and Upgrade decks in the exact order shown for the chosen player count:

| Players | Enemy Titans, in order | Enemy Upgrade cards, in order |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Crimson Knight, Impulse, Final Boss | Dracula's Blood, Power Potion, Berserker's Brew |
| 2 | Crimson Knight, Impulse, Chessmaster, Karate Guy, Final Boss | Dracula's Blood, Power Potion, Hermes's Boon, Shadow Step, Berserker's Brew |
| 3 | Crimson Knight, Impulse, Chessmaster, Karate Guy, Gunslinger, Sobek, Final Boss | Dracula's Blood, Power Potion, Hermes's Boon, Shadow Step, Sharpshooter, Basilisk's Fangs, Berserker's Brew |

Use these cards with the Solo & Co-op rules above. The publisher's [Modes and Challenges page](https://nerdlab-games.com/modes-and-challenges-final-titan/) lists additional challenges and their player-count variants.
